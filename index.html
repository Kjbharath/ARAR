ARAR GROUP is a premier holding company in the UAE, uniting leading specialized companies across Utility, Petrochemical, and Civil Infrastructure sectors.

With over 18+ years of industry excellence, our group brings together hundreds of expert engineers and professionals to deliver world-class EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) solutions. From complex high-voltage networks and vital petrochemical infrastructure to major road developments, we are committed to shaping the UAE's future with precision, safety, and unwavering quality.

Our Mission

To be the most trusted EPC partner in the UAE, delivering safe, innovative, and cost-effective power & infrastructure solutions that exceed client expectations and drive the nation's growth.

Our Vision

To shape the future of the UAE's infrastructure landscape as a leading diversified group spanning utility, petrochemical, and civil engineering across the GCC region.

Our Values

Excellence, accountability, and client-first commitment guide every decision — from tender to handover — ensuring lasting partnerships built on trust and results.
