Who We Are

Powering the UAE's
Electrical Infrastructure

ARAR Utility Company L.L.C is one of the UAE's foremost EPC contractors, specialising in high-voltage power distribution, underground cabling, substation construction, and street lighting. A proud subsidiary of the ARAR Group, we bring over 18 years of engineering excellence to every project.

We are an approved contractor with the Dubai Electricity and Water Authority (DEWA) and the Roads and Transport Authority (RTA). Our ISO-certified quality management system ensures every project meets the highest international standards of safety, precision, and delivery.

From complex 400KV EHV installations to intricate LV distribution networks, our team of expert engineers and technicians deliver turnkey electrical infrastructure solutions across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the wider GCC region.

What We Do

Core Electrical
EPC Services

End-to-end power infrastructure solutions — from underground cabling to complete substations, delivered with DEWA-grade precision.

Underground Cable Laying

LV, MV, HV & EHV underground cable installation up to 400KV, including full trenching, laying, jointing, and termination works across urban and highway corridors.

  • 33KV / 132KV / 400KV systems
  • HDPE duct installation
  • Cable jointing & termination
  • Trench backfilling & reinstatement

Substation Works

Complete design and construction of 11KV, 33KV, and 132KV substations — civil works, electrical installation, protection systems, and final commissioning.

  • DEWA-standard substations
  • Transformer installation
  • Switchgear & protection panels
  • SCADA & control systems

Street & Tunnel Lighting

Turnkey street lighting projects for highways, urban roads, bridges, and tunnels — including LED energy-efficient solutions that align with Dubai's smart city vision.

  • Highway & arterial roads
  • Tunnel & underpass lighting
  • LED smart control systems
  • Solar-integrated solutions

Cable Jointing & Termination

Specialist high-voltage cable jointing and termination services for both new installations and emergency repair works, using approved materials and certified technicians.

  • HV straight & transition joints
  • Outdoor & indoor terminations
  • Emergency repair 24/7
  • Fault location & testing

Dewatering Systems

Design and installation of dewatering systems for construction sites, cable trenches, and underground infrastructure, ensuring safe and efficient groundwater management.

  • Wellpoint dewatering
  • Sump pump systems
  • Deep well dewatering
  • Site drainage design

Civil & Landscaping Works

Integrated civil infrastructure works including cable trench systems, utility corridor construction, road reinstatement, and associated landscaping for project completeness.

  • Multi-duct cable trench systems
  • Road & pavement reinstatement
  • Utility corridor construction
  • Soft & hard landscaping
